listening server command:

python3 -m http.server <PORT> & cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:<PORT>
You can also use 'nc -lvnp <PORT>' instead of 'python3 -m http.server <PORT>'

all of the captured request will be received at the cloudflared tunnel listener tab.

if you are testing this on your own project or localhost, do this first before starting the listener
*** connect your web server to a cloudflared tunnel : cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost/<YOUR_FOLDER> ***
